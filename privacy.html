Privacy

This site has no analytics, no advertising tags, no tracking cookies, and no forms. It is a static page served by GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs under GitHub's privacy statement. Fonts load from Google Fonts.

What you send us

When you email us a request, a solicitation, or a line sheet, we use it to quote, fulfill, and invoice that order, and to keep the records that tax law and our public-sector contracts require. We do not sell it or use it for marketing lists.

Who sees it

Your request is shared only with the suppliers and carriers needed to fill it, and only the parts they need (for example, a part number and a ship-to address). We use business email, accounting, and automation tools to process orders, including AI tools that read incoming requests.

Questions or deletion requests

Email {{ site.contact }}. We will tell you what we hold and delete anything we are not required to keep.
